### 1. Critical Stages for Irrigation
Wheat requires 4 to 6 irrigations depending on soil type. The most critical stages are:
1. **Crown Root Initiation (CRI) stage**: 20 to 25 days after sowing (DAS). Most crucial; missing this reduces yield by 25-30%.
2. **Tillering stage**: 40 to 45 DAS.
3. **Jointing / Late tillering stage**: 60 to 65 DAS.
4. **Flowering stage**: 80 to 85 DAS.
5. **Milking stage**: 100 to 105 DAS.
6. **Dough stage**: 115 to 120 DAS.

### 2. Yellow Rust (Stripe Rust) Diagnosis & Control
- **Symptoms**: Yellow powdery pustules arranged in linear stripes along the leaf veins. When leaves are rubbed with fingers, yellow powder sticks to hands.
- In Hindi: पत्तियों पर पीली धारियां और पीले रंग का पाउडर निकलता है।
- In Marathi: पानांवर पिवळ्या रंगाचे पट्टे दिसतात आणि हाताला पिवळी पावडर लागते.
- **Control Measure**:
  - Spray Propiconazole 25% EC @ 1 ml per litre of water (200 ml in 200 litres of water per acre) as soon as initial symptoms appear.
  - Repeat after 15 days if rust persists.
  - Avoid excess nitrogen fertilizer application which aggravates rust.
