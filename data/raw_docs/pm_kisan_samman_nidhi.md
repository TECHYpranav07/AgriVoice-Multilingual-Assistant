# Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN) Guidelines
- **Publisher**: Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare, Government of India
- **Category**: Central Sector Government Scheme
- **Target Beneficiaries**: Small and Marginal Farmers across all States
- **Language**: English, Hindi, Marathi
- **Publication Date**: 2026-01-01
- **Approval Status**: Verified

### 1. Scheme Benefits
- Financial benefit of ₹6,000 per year is provided to eligible farmer families having cultivable landholding.
- Amount is transferred directly into Aadhaar-linked bank accounts in three equal installments of ₹2,000 every 4 months (April-July, August-November, December-March).
- In Hindi: पात्र किसान परिवारों को प्रति वर्ष ₹6,000 की वित्तीय सहायता तीन समान किश्तों में दी जाती है।
- In Marathi: पात्र शेतकरी कुटुंबांना दरवर्षी ₹६,००० चा लाभ तीन हप्त्यांमध्ये थेट बँक खात्यात जमा केला जातो.

### 2. Mandatory Requirements for Installment Credit
To receive PM-KISAN installments, farmers must complete:
1. **e-KYC**: Must be completed via OTP on PM-KISAN portal/app, or biometric authentication at CSC (Common Service Centre).
2. **Land Seeding (भूलेख अंकन / जमीन पडताळणी)**: Farmer's land records (Khasra/Khatauni or 7/12 extract) must be verified and seeded on the portal by state revenue officials.
3. **Aadhaar Bank Seeding**: Bank account must be seeded with Aadhaar and enabled for NPCI direct benefit transfer (DBT).

### 3. Exclusions
- Institutional landholders, government employees, income tax payees in previous assessment year, and pensioners with monthly pension above ₹10,000 are ineligible.
