# Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY) Crop Insurance Guide
- **Publisher**: Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare / PMFBY Directorate
- **Category**: Crop Insurance Scheme
- **Language**: English, Hindi, Marathi
- **Publication Date**: 2026-02-15
- **Approval Status**: Verified

### 1. Objectives and Coverage
PMFBY provides comprehensive insurance coverage against non-preventable natural risks from pre-sowing to post-harvest:
- Prevented sowing / planting risk.
- Standing crop loss due to drought, dry spells, flood, inundation, pests & diseases, landslide, natural fire, storm.
- Post-harvest losses (up to maximum 14 days after harvest for crops cut and spread for drying).
- Localized calamities (hailstorm, cloud burst, excessive unseasonal rain).

### 2. Farmer Premium Rates
The premium payable by farmers is extremely low and subsidized:
- **Kharif Food and Oilseed Crops**: Maximum 2.0% of Sum Insured.
- **Rabi Food and Oilseed Crops**: Maximum 1.5% of Sum Insured.
- **Annual Commercial / Horticultural Crops**: Maximum 5.0% of Sum Insured.
- Balance premium is shared equally between Central and State Governments.

### 3. Crucial Timeline for Claim Intimation (72-Hour Rule)
- For localized calamities or post-harvest loss, the farmer **MUST intimate crop loss within 72 hours** of the occurrence.
- Intimation channels:
  1. Crop Insurance App (फसल बीमा ऐप) on mobile.
  2. Toll-Free Number: 14447.
  3. Agriculture Officer / KVK or concerned Bank / Insurance company agent.
- In Hindi: प्राकृतिक आपदा से नुकसान होने पर ७२ घंटे के भीतर सूचना देना अनिवार्य है।
- In Marathi: नैसर्गिक आपत्तीमुळे नुकसान झाल्यास ७२ तासांच्या आत विमा कंपनीकडे किंवा Crop Insurance App वर तक्रार नोंदवणे अनिवार्य आहे.
