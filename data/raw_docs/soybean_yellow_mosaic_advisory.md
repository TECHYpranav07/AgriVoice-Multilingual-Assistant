# Advisory on Yellow Mosaic Virus Management in Soybean
- **Publisher**: ICAR - Indian Institute of Soybean Research (IISR), Indore
- **Crop**: Soybean (सोयाबीन)
- **Region**: Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, Rajasthan
- **Season**: Kharif
- **Language**: English, Hindi, Marathi
- **Publication Date**: 2026-07-10
- **Approval Status**: Verified

### 1. Symptoms & Transmission
- Yellow Mosaic Virus (YMV) causes bright yellow patches alternating with green areas on leaves. In severe cases, the entire leaf turns completely yellow and brittle.
- Seed formation and pod development are severely hampered.
- **Vector**: The virus is transmitted exclusively by Whiteflies (*Bemisia tabaci*), not by soil or mechanical touch.
- In Marathi: सोयाबीनमध्ये पिवळा मोझॅक हा पांढऱ्या माशीद्वारे पसरतो. पानांवर पिवळे पट्टे दिसू लागतात.
- In Hindi: यह रोग सफेद मक्खी द्वारा फैलता है और पत्तियों पर पीले धब्बे बन जाते हैं।

### 2. Prevention and Management
1. **Rogueing Out**: Immediately uproot and destroy virus-infected plants within the first 30 days of sowing to prevent spread to neighboring healthy crops.
2. **Yellow Sticky Traps**: Place 15 to 20 yellow sticky traps per hectare to monitor and trap whiteflies.
3. **Vector Control (Whitefly management)**:
   - Early stage: Spray Neem Oil 1500 ppm @ 3 ml per litre of water.
   - If whitefly population is high: Spray Thiamethoxam 25% WG @ 4 grams per 10 litres of water, or Acetamiprid 20% SP @ 2 grams per 10 litres of water.
   - **Safety Advisory**: Follow recommended dilutions strictly. Never apply chemical spray during noon heat or rain.
